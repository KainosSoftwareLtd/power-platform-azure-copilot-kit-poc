As a line manager, I want expense claims over £500 to be routed to me for
approval in Teams, so that high-value spend is reviewed before payment.

Acceptance criteria:
1. A claim over £500 is automatically sent to the submitter's line manager.
2. The manager can approve or reject from Microsoft Teams.
3. The submitter is notified of the outcome.
4. All approval decisions are auditable for 7 years.
5. Only managers can see claims from their direct reports.

Context: Model-driven app on Dataverse, Microsoft 365 licences.
